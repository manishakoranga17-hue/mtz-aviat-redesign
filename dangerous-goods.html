Service overview

Compliance is non-negotiable.

Dangerous-goods shipments fail when the paperwork fails. Menteiz operates a fully IATA-DGR compliant programme: every shipper’s declaration is checked, every package and label verified, and every acceptance signed off by a current-certified DG handler before it reaches the apron.

We handle all nine IATA hazard classes — corrosives, flammables, oxidizers, toxics, radioactives and beyond — including lithium batteries and overpacks under the latest DGR edition.

One audit-ready paper trail, one accountable interface, zero acceptance refusals.

  • Class 1Explosives (consultancy basis).
