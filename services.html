What we do

Ten cargo modes. One trusted hand.

From general freight and perishables to dangerous goods, valuables, live animals and active cold-chain — every cargo mode handled by trained specialists, with the right warehouse, the right paperwork and the right care.

  • 01

    General Cargo

    Textiles, spare parts, electronics, accessories, personal effects — reliable, cost-efficient point-to-point and interline handling with minimal transit time.

    Point-to-point · Interline
  • 02

    Perishable Cargo

    Cut flowers, fresh fruit and vegetables, frozen fish and shrimps, seeds — quality maintained origin to destination via temperature-controlled warehouse and transport.

    Temp-controlled
  • 03

    Dangerous Goods

    Highest standard for hazardous materials and restricted items — with designated storage, strict compliance, and trained handlers across all 9 IATA classes.

    IATA-DGR 1–9
  • 04

    Valuable Goods

    Jewellery, high-end electronics, artwork and other precious belongings — meticulously controlled at every step by trained specialists you can trust.

    High-value · Secure
  • 05

    Medical Equipment

    Reliable, cost-efficient handling for medical equipment and devices — direct point-to-point and interline routing with minimal transit and full traceability.

    Tracked · Compliant
  • 06

    Vulnerable Cargo

    Cameras, laptops, PDAs, mobile phones, watches and other valuable electronics — handled by trained specialists under a strictly controlled chain of custody.

    Chain of custody
  • 07

    Oilwell Equipment

    Specialised handling of oilfield hardware — expertise built around the unique demands of oil & gas and the project cargo it depends on.

    Oil & gas
  • 08

    Project Cargo

    From planning and route optimisation to clearance and final delivery — efficient, reliable and cost-effective for the most complex shipments.

    Outsize · Complex
  • 09

    Live Animals Cargo

    Specialised cargo mode for live animals — reliable, cost-effective routing with utmost care for welfare and timely delivery, point-to-point or via interline.

    Welfare-first
  • 10

    Temperature Control Goods

    Climate-controlled and temperature-sensitive cargo — precise temperature control end-to-end to keep quality and safety intact through transport and storage.

    Cold-chain
