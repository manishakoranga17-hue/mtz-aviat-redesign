Frequently asked

Project cargo, answered.

Quick answers on qualifying shipments, lead times, multi-modal projects, AN-124 / B747F charter, and risk management.

  • Anything that doesn’t fit standard ULD contours or requires multi-piece coordination — typically pieces over 1.6 m / 1,500 kg, or consignments where the dimensions/weight require engineering pre-shipment review.

  • As early as possible. For full-aircraft charter and outsize tender, 2–6 weeks of lead-time is typical; for AN-124 / B747F charter, longer. Engaging us at the RFP stage lets us influence dimensions and packaging for the best freight outcome.

  • Yes — air + road + sea-leg sequencing is standard. We coordinate trucking, lift-services and port handling at both ends through a vetted partner network with single-point accountability on the air leg.

  • Yes — via partner carriers. We’ll match the aircraft to the cargo-door spec, ULD requirements, and the airfield approvals at origin and destination, then issue a binding charter contract.

  • A pre-shipment risk register covers the route, the cargo profile, and the destination context. Per-shipment underwriting with specialist project-cargo underwriters is arranged before the first piece moves.
