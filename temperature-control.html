Frequently asked

Temperature control, answered.

Quick answers on CEIV Pharma, excursion handling, active ULDs, deep-frozen shipments, and temperature data delivery.

  • We operate GDP-aligned cold-chain lanes through CEIV-certified partner carriers and warehouses, with full audit trail. CEIV-certified end-to-end routings are available on request for regulated pharma shipments.

  • The on-call duty officer is alerted in real time. We isolate the shipment, log the excursion with timestamps and probe data, notify the shipper, and follow a documented response protocol — re-route, replenish dry-ice, or hold for review per shipper instruction.

  • Envirotainer (RAP / RKN / Releye), CSafe (RKN / RAP / Releye), Skycell, Va-Q-tec, and Pelican BioThermal. Lead time depends on the ULD type and route — typically 48–72 hours to secure equipment for hot lanes.

  • Yes — via specialised dry-ice-maintained passive shippers (Cryoport, BioLife Solutions) or LN2 dry shippers for bio-bank applications. Carrier acceptance and dry-ice replenishment is pre-cleared on every lane.

  • Every shipment carries calibrated probes that write a continuous record. On delivery a PDF temperature log is attached to the AWB; for repeat shippers we expose the live and historical data via API or our customer portal.
