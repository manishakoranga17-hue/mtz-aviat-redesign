Service overview

Temperature is non-negotiable.

Temperature-controlled cargo lives or dies by the cold chain. A single excursion outside spec can invalidate a pharma batch, spoil a high-value reagent, or destroy a vaccine lot. Menteiz operates GDP-aligned cold-chain lanes with validated equipment, monitored holds and a strict accept-or-explain protocol on every excursion.

From CRT (15–25°C) to chilled (2–8°C) to frozen (−25°C and below), we match ULDs, packaging and routings to the cargo’s thermal profile — before tender.

Every shipment lands at the right temperature with the data to prove it.

Temperature ranges

  • CRT (15–25°C)Controlled room temperature.
  • Chilled (2–8°C)Pharma, biologics, reagents.
  • Frozen (−25°C)Vaccines, plasma, dry-ice maintained.
  • Deep frozen (−80°C)Bio-samples, ultra-cold therapies.
  • Custom rangesAvailable via active ULDs on request.
