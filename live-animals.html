Frequently asked

Live animals, answered.

Quick answers on pet relocation, export health certificates, CITES species, equine sedation, and transit layovers.

  • Yes — for cat, dog and small-animal relocations we partner with IPATA-affiliated pet-shippers for door-to-door service. We handle the air leg under LAR; they handle pre-travel paperwork and home pickup/delivery.

  • The shipper’s veterinarian issues the export health certificate; we coordinate timing, endorse it with the Malaysian authority where required, and ensure it travels with the AWB and crate.

  • Yes, when the shipment is between accredited facilities (zoos, conservation programmes) and all CITES permits are issued. We don’t handle commercial wildlife trade and reserve the right to decline non-compliant tenders.

  • Sedation decisions are made by the shipper’s veterinarian on a per-animal basis. For racing and breeding stock we coordinate with the travelling groom and on-call vet to manage stress without sedation where possible.

  • We avoid long layovers wherever possible. Where unavoidable, we pre-arrange transit stabling/kenneling with welfare-graded partner facilities at major hubs — Dubai, Frankfurt, Singapore — and route around hubs without them.
