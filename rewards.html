Rewards Program

Give More, Earn More!

At Menteiz Aviation, we believe loyalty should be rewarded. Our Rewards Program is designed to provide added value to customers and partners who consistently choose us for their cargo operations.

Every shipment earns you rewards that can be redeemed for exclusive benefits, including cargo space vouchers on our aircraft routes to Kuching (KCH) and Kota Kinabalu (BKI).

How It Works
  • Earn reward points with every completed shipment
  • Redeem points for cargo space vouchers on selected routes
  • Enjoy exclusive incentives and priority privileges
  • Unlock greater rewards with higher shipment volumes
Exclusive Voucher Redemption

Customers can redeem reward vouchers for cargo space on our aircraft operating to:

Kuching (KCH) Kota Kinabalu (BKI)

These vouchers help reduce shipping costs while giving customers more flexibility and value for their cargo operations.
