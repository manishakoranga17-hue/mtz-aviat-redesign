Membership Tiers

Three tiers. One that fits your volume.

Every member starts on Silver and climbs automatically as annual volume grows. Higher tiers earn faster, redeem sooner, and unlock priority across the network.

  • Silver

    The entry tier for every new member. Standard voucher earn rate, member rates, and access to the rewards dashboard.

  • Gold

    For regular shippers. Boosted earn rate, priority handling on peak lanes, and a dedicated booking coordinator.

  • Platinum

    Our highest tier. Top earn rate, guaranteed capacity on key routes, partner privileges, and first-priority support, every time.

Program Terms

Earn rates, tier thresholds and voucher validity are confirmed at enrolment. Our team will tailor the program to your shipping profile.
