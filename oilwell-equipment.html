Quick answers on AOG turnaround, drilling chemicals, outsize equipment, offshore handover, and ATA Carnet support.

  • For Malaysian offshore: tender to delivery in 6–12 hours via direct freighter and crew-boat hand-off. For cross-border, 24–48 hours via next-flight-out routing on partner carriers.

  • Yes — muds, brines, completion fluids and additives across Classes 3, 6, 8 and 9 under IATA-DGR. Acceptance is pre-cleared with carriers; bonded transit available for cross-border movements.

  • Yes — PMC/PAG and freighter contour planning for items up to main-deck dimensions. For larger items (full BOP stacks, mud-system assemblies) we coordinate full-aircraft charter with cargo-door specs vetted before tender.
