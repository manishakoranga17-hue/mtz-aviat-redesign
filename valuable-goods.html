Frequently asked

Valuable goods, answered.

Quick answers on value caps, chain of custody, armed escort, insurance support, and booking discretion.

  • No fixed cap. High-value consignments above carrier-standard limits are pre-cleared with the carrier and underwriter before tender. Most jewellery and precious-metal lanes have unlimited declared value capacity.

  • Every transfer is signed, timestamped, geo-tagged and bound to the AWB. The audit log is available as a PDF on delivery and via API for repeat shippers.

  • Where local regulation requires or shipper requests, we coordinate licensed armed escort for ground legs. In several markets we can also arrange escorted aircraft acceptance.

  • We work with specialist marine and aviation underwriters for high-value consignments and issue coverage certificates before tender. Standard all-risk and named-peril policies available.

  • Shipment details are restricted to the operations team and the carrier’s acceptance officer. Manifests use generic descriptions where regulation permits. NDAs are standard for new high-value accounts.
