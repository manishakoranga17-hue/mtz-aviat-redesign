Our Story

19 years. One mission.

Menteiz Aviation was founded in [Year Founded] with a single conviction: that air cargo in Southeast Asia deserved better — more transparent, more reliable, and more connected than what the industry was offering. From a single office in Kuala Lumpur, our founders built the first piece of what would become a full-spectrum cargo network.

Over 19 years, we have grown alongside our partners — adding carriers, expanding routes, and building the technology to support cargo that matters most. Today, Menteiz handles over 150 million kilograms of freight annually, working with 200+ business partners across every continent.

Our mission has never changed: move the world’s most critical cargo faster, safer, and smarter than anyone else operating out of KUL. The tools have evolved. The commitment has not.

We built Menteiz to solve a problem we saw every day in this industry: cargo that moved without anyone really knowing where it was, or who was responsible. Every system, every certification, every relationship we’ve built over 19 years answers that one question: where is my cargo and is it safe?
