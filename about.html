Our story

Twenty years of moving the world’s freight — quietly, on schedule, on the metal that matters.

Menteiz Aviation Services has spent two decades on the apron, in the documentation room and on the carrier’s phone — building one of Southeast Asia’s most trusted air-cargo operators out of Kuala Lumpur. This is the story of how, why, and who.

Mission

One trusted solution for all your cargo.

We started with one belief: that air freight forwarding could be as deliberate, as accountable and as personal as a passenger flight. That belief shaped every standard we have built since — from how we screen carriers to how we acknowledge a customer at 03:14 in the morning.

Today Menteiz operates scheduled and charter freight lanes from Kuala Lumpur to every continent through 200+ partner airlines. We move general freight, perishables, dangerous goods, valuables, medical equipment, vulnerable shipments, oilwell hardware, project cargo, live animals and active cold-chain — the cargo other operators decline.

Our digital platform binds it together. From booking to delivery, every leg is connected, automated and visible.

Milestones

Two decades, one heading.

  1. 2005

    Founded in Kuala Lumpur

    A two-person ops desk, a phone line and one trusted carrier — that was the launch capital.

  2. 2009

    First charter season

    First fully chartered freighter contract for time-critical pharmaceutical cargo to the Middle East.

  3. 2013

    100 partner carriers

    Crossed our 100th partner-airline acceptance — opening direct lanes through Europe and Africa.

  4. 2017

    IATA-DGR full handling

    Built a hazardous-goods handling capability across all nine IATA classes, end-to-end.

  5. 2021

    Digital platform launch

    Quote, book, track, settle and reconcile — in a single, integrated operating console.

  6. 2025

    200+ carriers, 150M+ tonnes

    Twenty years on the apron, and the cargo keeps getting harder. We like it that way.
