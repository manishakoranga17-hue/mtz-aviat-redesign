What drives us

Mission & Vision.

Mission Statement

Move the world’s most critical cargo.

Faster, safer, and smarter — connecting shippers and carriers across every continent from our hub in Kuala Lumpur.

Vision Statement

A world where no cargo shipment is ever invisible.

Every piece, every pallet, and every aircraft move — tracked, transparent, and trusted.

What we operate by

Four standards. Every shipment.

01

Innovation

Fresh services, more choices for partners and shippers, and a willingness to retire process the moment something better is provable.

02

Exploration

New lanes, new commodity classes, new carrier relationships. We keep adding range so our customers don’t have to keep finding new freighters.

03

Reliability

Two decades on the apron, and a delivery record that earns repeat bookings. We say what we will do, then do it on the schedule we quoted.

04

Care

Live animals, organs in transit, hazardous freight, antiques and the cargo nobody wants to handle — all moved with the same deliberate hand.

Milestones

Two decades, one heading.

  1. 2005

    Founded in Kuala Lumpur

    A two-person ops desk, a phone line and one trusted carrier — that was the launch capital.

  2. 2009

    First charter season

    First fully chartered freighter contract for time-critical pharmaceutical cargo to the Middle East.

  3. 2013

    100 partner carriers

    Crossed our 100th partner-airline acceptance — opening direct lanes through Europe and Africa.

  4. 2017

    IATA-DGR full handling

    Built a hazardous-goods handling capability across all nine IATA classes, end-to-end.

  5. 2021

    Digital platform launch

    Quote, book, track, settle and reconcile — in a single, integrated operating console.

  6. 2025

    200+ carriers, 150M+ tonnes

    Twenty years on the apron, and the cargo keeps getting harder. We like it that way.
