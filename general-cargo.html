Frequently asked

General cargo, answered.

Quick answers on routing, transit times, customs, minimum weight, and how to book your first shipment.

  • Any non-specialised freight that doesn’t require temperature control, hazardous classification, or animal-welfare handling. Textiles, electronics, spare parts, personal effects and commercial samples all qualify.

  • We handle consignments from 5 kg upward on scheduled flights. Below that, we’ll route through a courier partner for cost-efficiency. No upper limit — full-aircraft charters available on request.

  • Most Asian destinations are 24–48 hours door-to-door. Middle East / Europe is typically 48–72 hours; North America 72–96 hours. We confirm an exact ETA at quote time.

  • Yes — export clearance from KUL is managed in-house under our AEO Malaysia status. At destination, we coordinate with vetted local brokers in 40+ countries on your behalf.

  • Send shipment details — origin, destination, dimensions, weight, timeline — via the contact form or directly to enquiry@mtz-aviat.com. We respond with a quote within operating hours and reserve capacity on confirmation.
