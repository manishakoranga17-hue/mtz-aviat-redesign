Service overview

General-purpose freight, moved with specialist care.

General cargo is the workhorse of the Menteiz network — everyday freight that needs to move quickly, cleanly and with full visibility. We handle it through our scheduled lanes and ad-hoc charter capacity, with 200+ partner carriers to choose from on every booking.

Every shipment is documented to IATA standards, weight-balanced for ULD optimisation, and routed point-to-point or via interline transfer to keep transit times short and rates competitive.

Whether you ship one carton or one freighter, we accept, route and track to the same operating standard.

  • Spare partsAutomotive, industrial, AOG-priority.
  • Consumer electronicsDevices, accessories, components.
