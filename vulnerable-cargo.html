Frequently asked

Vulnerable cargo, answered.

Quick answers on what differs from valuables, battery handling, product launches, shock events, and real-time tracking.

  • Valuable goods (bullion, jewellery, art) move under stricter armed-escort and vault protocols. Vulnerable cargo (consumer electronics) uses the same chain-of-custody discipline but at a different price point and capacity scale.

  • Devices with embedded or packaged lithium batteries are accepted under IATA-DGR (UN 3480/3481/3090/3091). State-of-charge is verified at acceptance and routings are pre-cleared with carriers that accept the relevant UN/PI.
