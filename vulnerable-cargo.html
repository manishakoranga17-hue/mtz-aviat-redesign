Service overview

Electronics that don’t forgive rough handling.

Vulnerable cargo lives between high-value and fragile. A dropped laptop is a written-off retail unit; a mis-routed phone is a stolen handset on the grey market. Menteiz handles these consignments through a dedicated lane — vetted handlers, tamper-evident packaging review, signature transfers, and shock loggers on every ULD.

Cameras, laptops, smartphones, tablets, smart watches and high-end audio — treated with the same deliberate care our valuable-goods lane applies to bullion.

One chain of custody from origin loading dock to final destination acceptance.
