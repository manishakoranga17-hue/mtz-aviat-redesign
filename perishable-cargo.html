Service overview

A cold chain you can actually see.

Perishable cargo is unforgiving — a missed connection or an unrefrigerated handover can write off an entire consignment. Menteiz protects every leg of the journey with cold-chain warehousing at KUL, temperature-monitored ULDs, and prioritised acceptance at destination.

From horticulture to frozen seafood, we’ve built our perishables programme to GDP-aligned standards with active-temperature ULD options, dry-ice handling, and direct hand-off to refrigerated ground transport.

Every shipment lands at the right temperature, on time, with a digital temperature log attached to the AWB.

Frequently asked

Perishables, answered.

  • Standard ranges: 2–8°C (chilled), 15–25°C (controlled ambient), and −18°C / −25°C (frozen). Custom ranges via active ULDs on request.

  • Every shipment carries a BLE or 4G logger that writes temperature, location, and shock events. Alerts fire on threshold breach, and a full audit log lands on the AWB at delivery.

  • We pre-arrange cold-storage acceptance at transit hubs, dry-ice top-ups where required, and last-built-first-loaded handling so perishables spend minimum time at ambient.

  • Yes — GDP-aligned handling for non-CEIV pharma, vaccines and clinical-trial material. CEIV-certified lanes available via partner carriers on request.
