Service overview

Critical equipment moves on schedule.

Medical equipment shipments don’t have a Plan B. Whether it’s a hospital MRI bound for an upgrade, a fleet of diagnostic devices to a new clinic, or single-piece replacement parts under warranty — Menteiz routes them on the carrier with the right cut-off and the right cabin temperature.

Our medical-equipment programme covers point-to-point and interline routing, vibration-sensitive handling, customs filings for restricted devices, and direct hand-off to ground transport with full traceability.

Every consignment is moved with the dignity its destination demands.
